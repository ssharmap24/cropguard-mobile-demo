Before connecting

  1. Connect your laptop and phone to the same trusted hotspot or private Wi-Fi.
  2. Start the CropGuard Flask server on your laptop.
  3. Enter the laptop's IPv4 address and port 5000.

Keep the laptop server running. Do not expose rover controls to public networks.
